The platform

Built for the entire product journey.

From receiving raw materials to shipping finished goods, LotNexus is being designed to bring critical operational workflows together.

01

ERP & Inventory

Item and partner records, stock transactions, lot-level inventory and connected business workflows.

02

Production & MES

Recipe-driven and manual production, material consumption, production lots and guided execution.

03

Warehouse Management

Storage locations, putaway, picking, stock transfers and operational warehouse workflows.

04

Traceability

Follow lot relationships across receiving, manufacturing, stock movement and outbound operations.

05

Quality Control

Quality holds, release decisions, traceable approvals and controlled movement eligibility.

06

Android PDA

Mobile scanning workflows designed for barcode-driven warehouse operations.

Designed for real operations

Practical workflows. Reliable traceability.

LotNexus focuses on clear operational steps, accountable decisions and the ability to understand where materials came from and where products went.

The platform is being developed for flexible deployment models, including customer-hosted and cloud-based environments.
