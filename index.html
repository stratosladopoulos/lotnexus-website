A few things to know

Before the launch.

A clear view of where the project stands.

Can I buy or download LotNexus now?

Not yet. LotNexus is in development and validation. This site introduces the product direction; it does not currently offer purchases, installer downloads or access to a hosted application.

Are the interfaces shown here real screenshots?

No. They are design illustrations created for this website. The numbers and records are fictional. Validated application screenshots will replace or supplement them when available.

How is the first release intended to be delivered?

The initial distribution plan is a Windows installer for customer-hosted environments. Final prerequisites, supported configurations, licensing and deployment options will be confirmed before release.

Will documentation be available in English?

Comprehensive English documentation is part of the release plan, covering operators, administrators, installation, backup, restore and upgrades. The outlines above show the intended coverage.
